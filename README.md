# LOOK · بُصّ

A short film for the 1 Billion Followers Summit AI Film Award 2027.

> Four months after the crash that killed his wife and daughter, a man keeps them alive at home as two mannequins, until the memory he has been refusing returns one fragment at a time, and he finally looks at the last thing his daughter wanted to show him.

**Running time 6:23 · 87 shots · one apartment, one car, four people, two mannequins.**

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [`LOOK-story-and-shot-list.pdf`](LOOK-story-and-shot-list.pdf) | Everything below in one PDF, for reading and review |
| [`story.md`](story.md) | Logline, the idea behind the film, its rules, characters, beat-by-beat story, full dialogue, sound plan |
| [`shot-list.md`](shot-list.md) | All 87 shots with timecodes, framing, camera, sound and dialogue; hero and cuttable shots marked |
| [`elements.md`](elements.md) | Higgsfield element tags and ids for every character and location, plus the job ids of both image sets |
| [`characters/`](characters/) | The seven character and mannequin sheets (front, back, face close-up) |
| [`assets.md`](assets.md) | Character, mannequin, location and prop briefs to build next, plus the generation risks |
| [`tools/build_shotlist.py`](tools/build_shotlist.py) | Source of the shot list. Edit a shot or a duration there and re-run it; timecodes are recalculated. |
| [`tools/build_pdf.py`](tools/build_pdf.py) | Rebuilds the PDF from the story, shot list and asset briefs |

To rebuild the shot list after an edit:

```bash
python3 tools/build_shotlist.py shot-list.md
```

## Award rules to confirm

These come from news coverage and third-party listings, not from the official rules page, which could not be opened from this workspace. **Check each one on 1billionsummit.com before production.**

| Rule | What was reported |
|---|---|
| Length | 5–7 minutes |
| Tool | Made mostly with Seedance (Dreamina / BytePlus) |
| Deadline | **20 November 2026** |
| Language | Any, with English subtitles |
| Subjects | Society, truth, technology, the planet, human connection |
| Entry | One film per person, 18+, made for the award |
| Not found | Judging criteria, exact share of Seedance footage, aspect ratio, file format, music and AI-disclosure rules |

The film fits "human connection" and "society": it is about grief, and about a man who finally lets someone in.

## Schedule to the deadline

If 20 November is right, there are nine days. Aim to submit on the 19th.

| Day | Work |
|---|---|
| 1 | Lock story and shot list. Character and mannequin sheets. |
| 2 | Apartment, car and road references. Props. The real crayon drawing. The 5-note tune. |
| 3–4 | A still keyframe for every shot. Review them in sequence for continuity before any video. |
| 5–6 | Video: the 21 hero shots first, then the mannequin-handling shots. |
| 7 | Remaining shots and pickups. Voices. |
| 8 | Edit, sound design, music, grade, the 4:3-to-wide pass. |
| 9 | Subtitles, quality check, export, submit. |

If time runs short, the four shots marked ○ in the shot list go first (13 seconds), and the film still lands above six minutes.
