# LOOK — Characters, Locations & Props to Build

The next step after the story is locked. Build these as reference sheets before generating a single shot; every shot in `shot-list.md` draws on them.

## Two looks

| | Present | Memory |
|---|---|---|
| Frame | 4:3 (full width from shot 77 on) | Full width |
| Camera | Tripod, locked, slow push-ins only | Handheld, close, breathing |
| Lenses | 35 / 50 / 85mm, deep-ish focus | 24–50mm, shallow focus, flares |
| Light | Closed curtains, practical lamps, dusty window glow | Low sun, golden to dusk |
| Colour | Sage, grey, faded wood. Yellow is the only saturated colour. | Warm amber, skin tones alive, blue sea line |
| Texture | Still air, dust, stale | Wind, movement, lens dirt |

Reference feel: naturalistic, photographic, 35mm film grain. Not glossy, not "AI cinematic". The apartment should look lived in by a real middle-class Cairo family, slightly dated, slightly cluttered.

## Characters

**ADAM, 38** (in almost every shot; the sheet that matters most)
- Egyptian, average build, slightly underweight now. Short dark hair going untidy, four months of not caring. Stubble, tired eyes, gentle face.
- **Healed scar at the right temple**, running into the hairline. Must be on every present-day close-up, and absent in the memory.
- Present wardrobe: one outfit for the whole film (it all happens in one day): a washed-out grey-blue shirt with rolled sleeves, dark trousers, bare feet or house slippers. Wedding ring on.
- Memory wardrobe: a clean light linen shirt, sunglasses pushed up on his head, shaved, healthier. He should look five years younger.
- Sheet needs: front / three-quarter / profile / back, neutral and crying, both wardrobes.

**LAYLA, 34** (memory only; her face in 6 shots, her hands in 3)
- Warm, expressive face, laugh lines. Shoulder-length dark wavy hair, loose in the car wind.
- A **burgundy cardigan** over a light summer dress: the same cardigan the mannequin wears. Wedding ring, a slim wristwatch (the one in the bag).
- Sheet needs: profile from the driver's side (her main angle), three-quarter looking back, laughing, and the look to the road.

**NOUR, 6** (memory only; 6 shots)
- Big dark eyes, a gap where a front tooth is missing, hair in two loose braids coming undone.
- A simple white summer top and shorts, **yellow sandals**. Not the yellow dress: that one was never finished.
- Always with the yellow crayon and the paper.
- Sheet needs: front, three-quarter, concentrating, beaming with the paper held up.

**MONA, 45** (2 shots, plus her voice through the door)
- Layla's older sister: same eyes and mouth, but clearly older. Grey streaks, hair tied back or a loose headscarf, glasses, a plain dark dress. Tired, kind. The audience must never mistake her for Layla.

**THE ADULT MANNEQUIN**
- A boutique display mannequin: matte off-white, smooth **abstract face with sculpted nose and brow but no painted eyes or lips**, visible seams at neck, shoulders and wrists, slightly scuffed. Seated-capable (it sits at the table and in the armchair) and also stands.
- A shoulder-length dark wig, slightly cheap. Layla's burgundy cardigan, buttoned, over a plain dress.
- It must read as an object in every frame: rigid, hard, hollow.

**THE CHILD MANNEQUIN**
- Same family as the adult: matte off-white, abstract face, seams. Size of a six-year-old.
- A dark wig with two bad braids. The **unfinished yellow dress**: visible pins, white tacking stitches, one sleeve missing. One yellow sandal buckled on.

## Locations

**The apartment** (one continuous set; build a floor plan and keep it)
1. **Kitchen**: small, older gas stove, a radio on the shelf, closed curtain over the window.
2. **Dining table**: beside a tall curtained window; a pendant lamp above. The symmetrical tableau frame (shots 08, 39) lives here.
3. **Living room**: sofa, armchair, a floor lamp, heavy curtains. Opens onto the sewing corner.
4. **Sewing corner**: machine, pincushion, fabric rolls, two mannequin stands.
5. **Hallway and entry**: long, narrow, front door at the far end with a chain; a console table beside it.
6. **Nour's room**: small bed, star-projector night-light, stuffed rabbit, crayon scribbles on the wall.

**The car**: a modest older family sedan, light interior. Build three fixed reference angles: from the back seat looking forward, driver's side looking at Layla, back seat on Nour.

**The road**: a two-lane desert highway toward the coast, low sun, a thin line of sea on the horizon.

**The landing**: a plain apartment stairwell outside the front door (one shot).

**Make the drawing for real.** Have a child draw it with crayons (three figures holding hands, sea, car, big yellow sun, no words), scan it, and use the scan for shot 76 and as the reference for every other appearance. It will be the most human thing in the film, and no model will draw it as well.

## Where AI generation will fight us

| Risk | Plan |
|---|---|
| Mannequins "come alive": blinking, breathing, turning heads | Abstract eyeless faces; start every shot from a locked still; state that the figure is rigid and inanimate; reject any take with the slightest movement. |
| Adam physically handling a mannequin (carry 15, dance 27, arm 30–32, carry 45) | The hardest shots. Stage wide, from behind or in near-silhouette. Schedule them early so there is time for retries. |
| Face drift on Adam across 60+ shots | One locked sheet, one wardrobe, the scar as an anchor. Check every take against the sheet. |
| A child's face in close-up, consistent across 6 shots | Short takes, one lighting condition, the same three car angles. |
| On-screen text | None, anywhere. Blank labels, no phone screens, no captions in the image. |
| The 4:3 crop | Generate natively in 4:3 if the model offers it; otherwise compose centre-safe in 16:9 and crop in the edit. |
| Lip-sync | Most lines play off-screen, on backs or in profile. Only five close-ups need it. |
